What's inside an image?

Besides pixels, image files can carry EXIF data (camera, lens, exposure, date, GPS), XMP packets from editing software, ICC color profiles and text comments.

Why metadata may be missing

Social networks, messaging apps and many editors strip metadata on upload or export. Screenshots and generated graphics often never had any. PixelPeek only shows what is really in the file.

Your privacy

Files are read by your browser, in memory. Nothing is uploaded, stored or tracked, and the page is technically blocked from making network requests.
